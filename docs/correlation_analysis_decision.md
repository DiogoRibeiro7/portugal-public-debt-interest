# Correlation Analysis Decision

The main paper excludes the previous level-correlation matrix. Pairwise correlations among interest expenditure, debt ratios, GDP growth, balances, yields, and implicit rates are weak evidence in a short annual macro-fiscal sample because the series are persistent, mechanically related, and often share GDP denominators.

The preferred interpretation is accounting-based. Changes in the interest burden are decomposed exactly into rate, average-debt-ratio, and interaction terms. Changes in the debt ratio are decomposed into the interest-growth term, primary balance contribution, and stock-flow residual.

Correlation tables may be useful as exploratory diagnostics, but they should not be presented as evidence for causal or structural drivers in the publication text.
